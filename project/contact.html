Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is your typical process for a new project?

    My process typically begins with an initial consultation to understand your requirements and goals. I then create wireframes and design mockups for your approval before moving to development. Throughout the project, I maintain regular communication and provide updates on progress.

  • How long does it take to complete a website?

    Project timelines vary depending on complexity and scope. A simple website might take 2-3 weeks, while more complex projects could take 1-3 months. I'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation based on your specific requirements.

  • Do you provide website maintenance services?

    Yes, I offer ongoing maintenance and support services to ensure your website remains secure, up-to-date, and functioning properly. Maintenance packages include regular updates, security checks, backups, and technical support.

  • What are your payment terms?

    I typically require a 50% deposit to begin work, with the remaining balance due upon project completion. For larger projects, I may arrange milestone payments. I accept payments via bank transfer, PayPal, or credit card.

  • Do you work with clients remotely?

    Yes, I work with clients globally and have established efficient remote workflows. We can communicate via email, phone, video calls, and project management tools to ensure smooth collaboration regardless of location.
